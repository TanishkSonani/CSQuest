# Lesson content

Lessons teach concepts directly through an accessible topic guide, key terms, a complete worked example and interactive practice. Learners can reopen guidance during a puzzle. Questions state their required inputs, assumptions, bit widths, signal conventions or circuit connections explicitly.

COA and Digital Logic include shared foundations that learners can revisit across related stages. Chapter maps and practice history identify topics by name. All 11 subjects are available; the current catalog and level counts are listed in the [README](../README.md).

Stable lesson IDs preserve scoring and progress during content updates. Milestone chests reflect completed lesson groups without implying an extra reward or action. See [lesson design](CONTENT_AUTHORING.md) and [interactive lessons](INTERACTIVE_LESSONS.md).
